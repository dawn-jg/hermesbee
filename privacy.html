Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

HermesBee is committed to privacy by design. This policy explains how our software and website handle your data.

1. Local-First Software

Our desktop tools and MCP servers run locally on your machine. Your documents, essays, notes, and search data never leave your computer — no cloud storage, no telemetry, no tracking.

2. Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK)

Products that use AI let you supply your own API key. Your key is stored only on your device and is used solely to call the API provider you choose (e.g., OpenAI, DeepSeek). We never see, store, or transmit your key.

3. Payment

All purchases are processed by Gumroad. We do not collect or store payment card information. Please refer to Gumroad's privacy policy for payment data handling.

4. Website

This website is a static page served by Cloudflare Pages. We do not use analytics cookies or personal data collection.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email admin@hermesbee.com.
